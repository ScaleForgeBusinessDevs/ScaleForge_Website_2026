# PRD 02 — Services Index Page

**URL:** `/services`
**Build target:** Production page using the locked design system.

---

## Page identity

The Services index owns **WHAT ScaleForge sells**. It's the menu. A buyer scans this page to understand the full scope of what they can engage ScaleForge for, then dives into the relevant detail page.

This page does **not** sell. It directs. Selling happens on the homepage (emotionally), on Solutions (philosophically), on each service detail page (specifically), and on Pricing (commercially).

The page must:
1. Lead with AI Development as the flagship service (repositioning).
2. Show all five service tracks with enough detail to identify the right one in <10 seconds.
3. Route buyers to detail pages cleanly.

---

## Target user & intent

- **Primary**: Buyer who knows they want help but isn't sure which service track applies — needs disambiguation.
- **Secondary**: Buyer doing comparison shopping across multiple agencies who wants to see ScaleForge's full menu.
- **Search intent:** "web design and seo agency," "ai development agency," "full service digital agency"

---

## What this page owns (vs. what it doesn't)

| Owns | Does NOT own |
|---|---|
| The 5-service inventory and how they connect | Pricing (→ Pricing) |
| Service categorization and routing | Methodology (→ Solutions) |
| One-paragraph positioning per service | Process and deliverables in depth (→ Service detail pages) |
| The "everything works in tandem" narrative | Industries (→ Solutions) |

---

1. **Hero** — Establish full-service scope.
