# PRD 11 — About Page

**URL:** `/about`
**Build target:** Production page using the locked design system.

---

## Page identity

The About page owns **WHO** — the team behind ScaleForge, the founders, the formation story, and the values that bind the work.

This is the trust page. International buyers (US/UK/AU) evaluating a Pakistan-based agency want to see the humans behind the brand. Real names, real faces, real titles, real backgrounds. The About page is also where the most skeptical buyer goes to close the trust gap before booking.

This page does NOT pitch services or methodology — those live elsewhere. About is about the people and the story.

---

## Target user & intent

- **Primary**: High-intent buyer doing final due diligence before booking a call.
- **Secondary**: Press, partners, or potential hires evaluating the company.
- **Search intent:** "[ScaleForge] team," "[ScaleForge] founders," "about ScaleForge," "[founder name]"

---

## What this page owns

| Owns | Does NOT own |
|---|---|
| The formation story | The methodology (→ Solutions) |
| Founder bios with full names, photos, titles | Service narrative (→ Services) |
| Team values (as people, not as deliverables) | Project proof (→ Work) |
| Company credentials, formal registration, location | Pricing (→ Pricing) |

---

## Section flow

1. **Hero** — Position the page as a story, not a brochure.
2. **The story** — How ScaleForge was formed and why.
3. **The founders** — Two profile blocks with full bios.
4. **What we believe** — Personal values translated to operating principles.
5. **Where we work from** — Location, time zone, credentials.
6. **Open positions** — If actively hiring; otherwise omit or replace with "Work with us" pathway.
7. **CTA** — Direct path to start a conversation.

### 2. The Story

**H2:** How ScaleForge Came to Be

**Body (3–5 paragraphs):**

ScaleForge started with a frustration. After years of watching ambitious businesses pay agencies for slow WordPress sites, generic SEO retainers, and disappearing freelancers, Shahood and Ruhan decided to build the studio they wished existed — one that did the work properly, owned its outcomes, and treated every engagement like a partnership.

In 2024, the two co-founded ScaleForge as a formal partnership under Pakistan's Companies Act 2017, structured as a two-founder firm with a clear ownership split and operating mandate. From the beginning, the bet was the same: combine elite hand-coded engineering with aggressive search visibility, do the work for a deliberately small cohort each season, and let the receipts compound.

Today, ScaleForge operates as a remote-first studio based in Karachi, serving clients across the US, UK, Australia, and the EU. Every engagement is led directly by the founders or a senior team member they've trained — no account managers, no telephone game, no diluted execution.

The studio is in active transition toward AI Development & Automation as a flagship service, building bespoke automation systems using n8n, Make.com, Vapi, Notion, and Google Sheets alongside the original Next.js web engineering and SEO work that built the firm.

## Notes

- **The founder photos must be high quality.** Current site assets work; if a refresh is feasible, slightly more cinematic portraits (matching the brand's premium positioning) would elevate the page.
- **Mention of Pakistan and Karachi early in the page** is intentional — buyers find it eventually anyway, so leading with transparency builds more trust than burying it. Be proud, not apologetic.
- **The "What We Believe" section** is the highest-soul part of the page. The phrasing should sound like a person, not a brand guidelines document. Avoid corporate value-words like "Excellence," "Innovation," "Integrity" — they're meaningless.
- **The Companies Act 2017 mention** is a credibility signal for international buyers who want to verify the firm is legally registered.
- **Process / "How We Build Exceptional Software" is intentionally removed from this page** — it duplicates content from Solutions and the homepage. About owns WHO, not HOW.
