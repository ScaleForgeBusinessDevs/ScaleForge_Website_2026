# PRD 01 — Solutions Page

**URL:** `/solutions`
**Build target:** Production page using the locked design system.

---

## Page identity

The Solutions page owns **HOW ScaleForge thinks and works**. Not the why (that's Home). Not the what (that's Services). The methodology, the development DNA, the principles that govern every engagement.

A buyer should leave this page understanding *how ScaleForge is structurally different from other agencies* — not just that we're better, but specifically what's different about our approach to code, search, scale, client relationships, and quality control.

This is the "philosophical" page. It's also the page that converts the technically-minded buyer (the CTO, the founder who knows code) into a believer.

---

## Target user & intent

- **Primary**: Founders / marketing leads who have already read the homepage and want to validate that ScaleForge is technically credible before booking a call.
- **Secondary**: Engineers and CTOs evaluating whether ScaleForge's approach matches their stack and standards.
- **Search intent**: "how does [agency] work," "custom code vs templates," "agency methodology," "next.js agency"

---

## What this page owns (vs. what it doesn't)

| Owns | Does NOT own |
|---|---|
| Development DNA / philosophy | Service tier pricing (→ Pricing) |
| The "why most agencies fail" thesis | Team bios (→ About) |
| Core principles (Performance, Transparency, Long-Term Thinking, Quality) | Specific service deliverables (→ Services) |
| Industries served | The brand origin story (→ About) |
| Methodology overview | Project outcomes (→ Work) |

---

## Section flow

1. **Hero** — Position the page: "Built to dominate. Built to help you win."
2. **Why most agencies fail you** — The thesis section. Establish the problem.
3. **Our mission** — One paragraph, sharp.
4. **Our core development DNA** — Six principle blocks (the heart of the page).
5. **Guiding values** — Four-tile grid of values.
6. **Industries we've worked with** — Versatility proof.
7. **CTA** — Limited cohort framing, dual buttons.

### 4. Our Core Development DNA

**Eyebrow:** WHAT MAKES SCALEFORGE STRUCTURALLY DIFFERENT

**H2:** Our Core Development DNA

This is the heart of the page. Six principle blocks, each with a small visual demonstration (existing components from homepage design system: code snippet, score meter, ranking ladder, traffic graph, roster slots, live update feed).

#### Block 1 — Core Philosophy
**Title:** Bespoke Speed. Complete Code Safety.

**Body:**
Every project we take on is built from scratch — no page builders, no WordPress themes, no borrowed components. We write clean, typed TypeScript from line one, which means your website is faster, safer, and owned entirely by you. Our code doesn't break when a plugin updates. It doesn't slow down when traffic spikes. It simply performs.

**Visual element:** Code snippet card showing `type ClientSite = { ownership: '100%'; security: Readonly<T>; performance: 'blazing' }` with `✓ Type-safe ✓ Zero errors`

#### Block 2 — Zero Templates
**Title:** No Libraries or Themes Bloated with Markup.

**Body:**
Pre-built themes ship with thousands of lines of CSS and JavaScript you'll never use. That dead weight silently kills your PageSpeed scores and handicaps your Google rankings. We design and develop every component purposefully — each pixel earns its place. The result is a website that loads in under 1.5 seconds on mobile, with a score your competitors simply cannot match.

**Visual element:** PageSpeed score dial showing `100 PERFECT` with `LCP < 1.5s | CLS: 0`

#### Block 3 — Semantic Growth
**Title:** Long-Term Organic System Visibility.

**Body:**
Rankings built on shortcuts disappear overnight. We architect your website's semantic HTML structure, schema markup, and content hierarchy so search engines can crawl, understand, and trust your pages completely. Every heading, every internal link, and every piece of metadata is a deliberate signal to Google — compounding your authority month after month.

**Visual element:** Ranking ladder showing progression `#47 → #23 → #11 → #4 → #1`

#### Block 4 — Scale First
**Title:** Designed Strictly for Growth Acceleration.

**Body:**
We don't build websites for where your business is today. We build them for where your business is heading. Modular React architectures mean new pages, features, and integrations slot in without a full rebuild. Whether you're going from 100 visitors a month to 100,000 or expanding into new service markets, your platform scales with you.

**Visual element:** Visitor count graph showing growth from `100 → 100,000+` with caption `↑ No ceiling. No rebuild.`

#### Block 5 — Client Focus
**Title:** Limited Cohorts for Deep, Precise Output.

**Body:**
Most agencies spread themselves across 50 active clients, which means your project gets a fraction of the attention it deserves. We deliberately cap our active roster each season. When you partner with ScaleForge, you get a dedicated team that has studied your industry, analyzed your competitors, and mapped out exactly what it will take to rank and convert.

**Visual element:** Season roster grid showing `4 / 6 slots filled — 2 remaining. Roster resets each season.`

#### Block 6 — Work Culture
**Title:** Elite Talent. Collaborative Execution.

**Body:**
Our team is built around two principles: deep technical excellence and relentless communication. We don't disappear after onboarding. You receive regular progress updates, milestone walkthroughs, and a direct line to your strategist at every stage. No account managers playing telephone. Just the people doing the work, keeping you informed.

**Visual element:** Live project update feed showing timestamped milestones
